# Welcome to My Blog: A Developer's Journey 🚀

Hello, world! 👋 I'm **Ajran Hossain**, a passionate **Dynamics 365 Business Central Developer** and lifelong learner, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. This blog marks the beginning of an exciting journey where I'll be sharing my experiences, insights, and projects with the developer community.

## Who Am I?

I'm a **Microsoft Dynamics 365 enthusiast** with over a year of professional experience in building, customizing, and deploying ERP solutions. My journey began with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science & Engineering from the **American International University – Bangladesh**, where I cultivated my love for technology and problem-solving.

Currently, I specialize in **AL programming**, crafting modules that enhance business efficiency, and integrating third-party systems to deliver seamless user experiences. Alongside my professional work, I'm a tinkerer who enjoys exploring new technologies like **Next.js**, **Laravel**, and **ASP.NET** to build innovative solutions.

## Professional Highlights

In my current role as a **Dynamics 365 Developer** at Berger Tech Consulting Ltd., I've:

- Developed and deployed custom ERP modules, including Attendance, Appraisal, and Payroll systems.
- Integrated external APIs and automated workflows to streamline processes.
- Crafted solutions using **SharePoint**, **ASP.NET**, and **AngularJS** to enhance collaboration and operational efficiency.

Beyond work, I take pride in contributing to the tech community through open-source projects and a publication on **melanoma detection using CNNs**, which is dear to my heart.
